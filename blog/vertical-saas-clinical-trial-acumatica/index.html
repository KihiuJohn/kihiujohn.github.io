Clinical Trial Management Vertical on Acumatica

Clinical Trial Management Vertical on Acumatica is the pattern that defines the next decade of enterprise software.

John KihiuJun 30, 202612 min read

Clinical trials are the most specialized vertical in this series, and the one where I most want to be upfront: there is no such thing as running a clinical trial "on Acumatica." A trial needs a validated Clinical Trial Management System (CTMS) and electronic data capture (EDC) system, subject randomization, and regulatory submission tooling, none of which Acumatica offers or should be asked to approximate. What Acumatica can legitimately do is sit underneath the trial sponsor's or CRO's (contract research organization's) financial operations — investigational product (IP) inventory and site payment tracking.

CTMS and EDC own the trial itself

Subject enrollment, randomization, visit scheduling, adverse event reporting, and case report form data collection are the job of dedicated, typically GxP-validated CTMS/EDC platforms — software built and validated specifically for FDA/ICH-GCP requirements around electronic records and signatures in a clinical trial context. Acumatica has no subject data model, no randomization capability, and no GxP validation posture for this purpose. This is not a gap to fill with customization; it is a different category of software entirely.

Where it fits: investigational product inventory and site financials

Investigational product — the trial drug or device — has to be tracked from manufacture or receipt, through shipment to trial sites, to dispensing/administration and eventual reconciliation or destruction, with full chain of custody. That is structurally the same lot-genealogy problem as pharma manufacturing and distribution covered earlier in this series, just with trial-specific metadata layered on: which site, which protocol, which arm (if unblinded at the sponsor's operational level — many trials keep site-level shipment tracking separate from the blinding of clinical outcome data).

CONFIG · IP SHIPMENT TO TRIAL SITE
Stock Item:          IP-PROTOCOL-4471-A
Lot/Serial Class:     Track Expiration = Yes, Issue = Expiration (FEFO)
Custom Fields:         Protocol Number, Site ID, Kit Number
                       (kit number is the trial-specific identifier;
                        Acumatica's internal lot key stays separate)
Transaction:           Transfer Order, Warehouse (depot) -> Site
Reconciliation:        Site periodically reports used/unused/
                       destroyed kits back via a scoped integration;
                       Acumatica records the reconciling inventory
                       adjustment with reason code

The chain-of-custody trail this produces — receipt, shipment to site, reconciliation, destruction — is genuinely useful supporting evidence for a trial's drug accountability audit, but it supplements the CTMS's own records; it does not replace the GxP-validated system of record for the trial itself.

Site payments and sponsor/CRO financials

Separately, and more straightforwardly, a sponsor or CRO has ordinary ERP needs: paying trial sites per a payment schedule tied to enrollment and visit milestones, tracking budget versus actual spend per protocol (Acumatica's Project module fits this — protocol as project, site as task or cost center), and multi-currency handling for international multi-site trials. This is closer to conventional project accounting than anything healthcare-specific.

Do not let subject-level data anywhere near Acumatica

Clinical trial subject data is about as sensitive as healthcare data gets, often with its own consent and de-identification requirements independent of general HIPAA. There is no legitimate reason for subject-identifiable information to appear in Acumatica in this vertical — even the exceptions discussed elsewhere in this series (private-pay AR, for instance) do not apply here. If a proposed integration would pass subject-level data into the ERP, that is a design error to catch in review, not a customization request to fulfill.

Wrapping up

Clinical trial management belongs entirely to validated CTMS/EDC systems — Acumatica has no role there and no business approximating one. Its legitimate, narrow fit is investigational product chain-of-custody inventory (leaning on the same lot-genealogy strength as pharma manufacturing) and ordinary project-based financials for site payments and protocol budgets, with subject-level data never crossing into the ERP under any circumstance.
