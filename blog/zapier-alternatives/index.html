Zapier vs n8n vs Make for ERP — A Comparison

Zapier, n8n, and Make solve the same problem with different trade-offs. For ERP work the deciding factors are pricing model, self-hosting, and how far each bends before it breaks.

John KihiuJun 27, 202612 min read

Zapier is the default, but it is not the only automation platform, and for ERP work the alternatives — n8n and Make — differ in ways that matter. The right choice is not about which is "best" but which fits your volume, your control needs, and your team's appetite for infrastructure. Here is how they actually compare for ERP automation.

The trade-offs that matter

PricingPer task — climbs with volumeSelf-host (free) or cloud; not per-taskPer operation — cheaper than Zapier at volume

Where each fits

Zapier wins on breadth of native apps and speed to build — best for a wide range of low-to-moderate volume automations where getting live fast matters more than per-run cost. n8n wins when you need control: self-hosting for data residency and cost, full access to the ERP's API, code when you need it, and queue-mode scaling for high volume. Make sits between them — a strong visual builder that is cheaper than Zapier at volume, good when you want more power than Zapier without running infrastructure.

The ERP angle

For ERP specifically, two factors tilt the decision. Data control: ERP data is sensitive, and n8n's self-hosting keeps it inside your boundary, which some organisations require. Volume and cost: ERP event streams can be high, and Zapier's per-task model gets expensive there, where n8n's self-hosted model or Make's per-operation pricing is cheaper. If neither is a constraint, Zapier's ease often still wins.

You don't have to pick just one

Many teams run Zapier for the sprawling long tail of simple automations where its ease and app catalogue shine, and n8n for the few high-volume, data-sensitive ERP workflows where self-hosting and control matter. Matching the tool to each workload beats forcing everything onto one platform for the sake of consistency.

Zapier, n8n, and Make are the same idea at different points on the ease-versus-control curve. For ERP automation, weigh pricing model, self-hosting, and technical ceiling against your actual volume and data requirements — and consider using more than one, Zapier for breadth and n8n for the heavy, sensitive flows. Pick per workload, not per brand.
