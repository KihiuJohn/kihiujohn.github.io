Built from the integration, not from a slide.

These demos were assembled from the same fields, actions and response objects I work with when customising Acumatica for fiscalisation: header fields, line classification, tax mapping, device registration and the authority response written back to the document.

The screen is deliberately familiar to anyone who has used a modern ERP. The branding, colours, company names, TINs, SKUs, prices and responses are all fictional. Nothing here is copied from a client tenant or a live authority portal.

If you are evaluating a fiscalisation partner, this is the closest thing to a sandbox without giving anyone access to your tenant.

Validation is an action, not a pageThe invoice stays in context. The connector runs pre-flight checks, signs the payload and writes the receipt back.
The payload is inspectableOpen the request and response tabs. You can see exactly which fields an authority accepts and what comes back.
The print layout is part of the jobA fiscal integration is not done until the customer-facing document carries the receipt, signature and QR correctly.
